About me

I have always been fascinated by how the human mind works: how we think, how we react, how we get back on our feet. I am a psychodynamic psychotherapist and for many years I have also worked as an emergency psychologist, alongside rescuers and communities hit by floods and other major critical events.

Emergency work taught me something I bring into the practice every day: under pressure, the deepest mechanisms in each of us come to the surface, the ones that stay silent in everyday life. That is where the best and the most fragile parts of a person meet, and where one can truly understand and change.

Where I learned to observe human beings

Not only in emergencies. In rugby I learned that what counts is not how hard you fall but getting up again, and that what often makes the difference is a teammate who offers a hand, sometimes just with a glance. Underwater I learned the value of breath, of self-control and of the person beside you. In the mountains I understood that you have to listen to your limit, and that sometimes the strongest choice is to turn back.

In therapy I try to be that teammate: I do not play the game for you, but I am there when it is time to get up. I believe in a psychotherapy that helps you change while remaining yourself: without erasing anything of who you are, but learning to make it work better. It is a principle I also learned on my own path. Every consistent choice has a price, and it is worth paying.

I see people in my practice in Rome, in Italian, English and French.

Training and experience

  • Clinical and community psychologist, registered with the Order of Psychologists of Lazio, no. 19137 (since 2012)
  • Specialisation in psychoanalytic psychotherapy of adults and children, SIRPIDI – ISTEBA, Rome (2016)
  • Emergency psychologist: interventions in major emergencies, including the floods in the Marche (2022) and Emilia-Romagna (2023); certified to operate from helicopters
  • SIPEM trainer for colleagues approaching emergency psychology; seminars for Civil Protection and SIPEM
  • Member of the national Technical Scientific Committee of SIPEM SoS Federazione
  • Lecturer and thesis supervisor in the master’s programme in emergency psychology of the Consorzio Humanitas
  • Designer of five training exercises with the Volunteer Fire Brigades of Trentino in Lavarone
  • Long experience in the field of disability
