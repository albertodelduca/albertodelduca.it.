For those living or working abroad

Living or working far from home, moving between relocations, assignments and returns, can be demanding in ways that are not always easy to put into words for the people around you. Distance, irregular rhythms, being far from loved ones, the weight of decisions made in complex settings: I know these situations well, because I have worked for years with people under heavy pressure, including in international settings.

Online sessions, when you cannot come to the practice

If you are abroad, or travel often, we can work remotely by video call. Sessions last 45 minutes, as in the practice, and can be held in Italian, English or French. The first meeting can last up to an hour and a half.

For now I work remotely with people who are in a euro-area country. If you live elsewhere, please write to me anyway and we will see together what is possible.

Between one assignment and the next

If your rhythms are irregular, a fixed weekly appointment may not be realistic. We can agree on a schedule that takes your travel and time zones into account, alternating, when you are in Rome, between sessions at the practice and online sessions.

What you need to begin

  • A stable connection and a place where you can speak without being overheard.
  • A device with camera and microphone, and a time agreed in advance.
  • A first meeting in which we assess together whether remote work suits your situation.

Fees are the same as at the practice, and we agree on how to arrange payment at first contact. Professional secrecy and confidentiality apply as they do in the practice; for details you can read the privacy notice (in Italian).

Online work is not suitable for emergencies. If you or others are in immediate danger, call the emergency number of the place where you are (112 in Italy and across the European Union).
